# Assessing LLM-Generated Feedback for OSCEs at UW-Madison

Pilot materials for a talk at the IHER Conference (September 15, 2026) by Dylan Pieper, MA, Research Analyst.

## Disclaimer

**Use at your own risk.** These are pilot materials shared for discussion and transparency only. They are **not for production use** — the prompt, outputs, and findings here have not been validated for use in student assessment, grading, or any decision affecting a student's academic standing.

These materials are **not officially endorsed by the University of Wisconsin–Madison**, its School of Medicine and Public Health, or any of its programs. Views and content are the author's own and do not represent an institutional position.

LLM-generated feedback can be inaccurate, inconsistent, or biased. Any use of these materials, or of the approach they describe, is solely at the user's own risk, without warranty of any kind. Do not submit protected health information, student records, or other confidential data to a language model without appropriate institutional review and approval.
